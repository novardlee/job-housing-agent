# Project and support brief

Job & Housing Agent grew from a personal project that coordinates job discovery, truthful tailored applications, interview invitations and preparation, employer replies, and housing tied to realistic income and commute. Its owner reports that the workflow has worked so far and is sharing the instruction structure developed with an AI assistant.

The distinctive contribution is the correction system: later facts override stale prompts, false claims do not propagate through resumes, separate watchers avoid duplicate work, and housing decisions follow employment geography. The public package makes those patterns reusable without publishing the owner's personal dataset.

## What is available now

One consolidated agent prompt, explicit invitation function, mode boundaries, categorized correction history, configuration templates, output contracts, and evaluation cases. The private source contains working artifacts and dated scripts. Portable connectors, an application runtime, and independent public end-to-end results are not included in this release.

The package also preserves career evaluation and Indeed/ZipRecruiter profile building. The [aggregate personal case study](RESULTS.md) identifies 15 conservative interview opportunities and two recorded offer outcomes; a broader total of 18 records also includes two platform intro-call invitations and one related-role discussion. Detailed evidence remains private. Do not advertise these as attendance totals, authenticated offer letters, guaranteed results, or agent-only causation.

## Proposed support milestones

1. Publish a reproducible synthetic demonstration in a selected assistant host.
2. Extract portable file/document/state utilities from the dated private scripts without personal data.
3. Build and test scoped email/browser adapters, invitation/reply deduplication, and recovery after locked files or interrupted runs.
4. Improve accessibility, phone-friendly handoffs, setup guidance, and maintenance.

Budget amounts and funding recipients remain the owner's decisions. Do not present contributions as guaranteed financial returns or promise a job/apartment outcome. Record any measured result with dates, evidence, costs, limitations, and permission to publish.

Potential support models include maintenance sponsorship, setup/support services, or a hosted product after implementation. Choose licensing and a validated offer before adding pricing or a funding link.

## Earning through GitHub

GitHub can make the project discoverable and show the workflow to potential users. Posting a repository alone does not create sales or sponsorships.

- **Sponsorship:** eligible contributors can set up GitHub Sponsors and sponsorship tiers. See [GitHub's account setup instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-personal-account) and [eligibility overview](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors).
- **Paid setup/customization:** a proposed offer is configuring the workflow around a customer's own profile, job targets, invitation channels, and housing criteria. GitHub serves as the demonstration; customer acquisition and payment need a separate business process.
- **Hosted access:** a possible later offer is ongoing access to a maintained service. This requires a working hosted product, operating cost model, and appropriate user-data handling; it is not present in this package.

Suggested first commercial experiment: show a sanitized demonstration, offer a bounded setup service, and validate whether anyone will pay before investing in hosting. This is a recommendation to test, not evidence of demand or a forecast of income.

## Proposed path to profitability

**First customer:** a job seeker who needs help translating existing experience into suitable roles, accurate platform profiles, and an organized application/interview workflow. Housing comparison is optional when it is relevant to the job search. This target audience is a hypothesis to validate.

**First paid offer:** one private setup with a career evaluation, Indeed/ZipRecruiter copy packs, confirmed profile and search policy, a demonstration job queue, and an interview-preparation handoff. Agree a delivery date and one defined revision round. Sell the setup and support delivered, never a promised number of interviews or offers. The [pilot scope](SETUP_OFFER.md) defines exclusions.

**GitHub's role:** demonstrate the instructions and fictional sample, explain the personal aggregate results honestly, and provide one private inquiry route once the owner selects it. Track inquiries, qualified prospects, and paid customers separately; stars and downloads are not revenue. No contact route, checkout, or outreach campaign is active yet.

**Pilot sequence:** prepare one complete synthetic sample in a selected host; discuss the offer with interested users through an owner-approved channel; then deliver up to three separately agreed paid pilots, one at a time. Record setup hours, revision/support hours, direct tool costs, customer feedback, and payments actually received. Do not commission custom hosting until this experiment supports it.

**Pricing method:** establish the owner's minimum acceptable hourly compensation and estimated delivery/support time before quoting. A planning floor is `(delivery hours + support hours) × target hourly compensation + direct costs + allocated overhead`; adjust for the actual payment fee arrangement. This is an internal estimate, not a published price. After each pilot, calculate payment received minus refunds, fees, direct costs, labor allowance, and overhead. Positive cash receipts alone do not establish profit.

**Go/no-go:** continue the offer only if customers accept its price, the agreed work is delivered reliably, and the margin remains positive after counting the owner's time. Otherwise narrow the deliverables, improve the process, or retest pricing. Review after the first three pilots; this small sample does not prove broad demand.

**Later revenue:** offer separately scoped profile refreshes or interview preparation when customers request them. Consider recurring maintenance only after measuring recurring work and costs. A hosted subscription requires a tested product and support model. Sponsorship can support public maintenance, but the plan does not depend on donations.

**Next owner decisions:** supported host, private contact method, payment channel, delivery capacity, hourly compensation target, and pilot price. These are launch decisions; this plan does not activate sales or publish the repository.
