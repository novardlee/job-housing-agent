# Recorded personal results — aggregate case study

The owner's saved records from **June–September 2026** contain **15 distinct role-specific interview opportunities and two job-offer outcomes**, using the conservative counting method below. The workflow supported job discovery, profiles, applications, invitation tracking, preparation, career evaluation, and housing decisions.

| Metric | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Conservative interview-opportunity count | **15** | Distinct employer/role opportunities, including recruiter screens; not interviews attended |
| Broader opportunity/invitation/discussion records | **18** | The conservative set plus two platform hiring-manager intro-call invitations and one related-role discussion |
| Offer outcomes recorded | **2** | One log-supported written-offer record; one user-reported offer with no written record found |

An opportunity or invitation may be cancelled, postponed, unconfirmed, or declined. A platform intro queue does not guarantee a full employer interview. A related-role discussion is included only in the broader convention. Repeat rounds are not additional distinct employer/role opportunities.

## Evidence levels

The written-offer record is described in saved logs and a private decision memo. The second offer was reported by the user; its format was presumed verbal/in person in the records. This packaging review did not authenticate original letters or inspect live employer messages.

The user also applied, booked interviews, attended them, and made decisions. These results occurred during the personal workflow; they do not establish that the agent originated every opportunity or caused the offers. No attendance total, acceptance status, hiring conversion rate, or outcome guarantee is claimed. The public package has not reproduced these results for another user.

## Counting method

Start with 21 date-prefixed roster rows. Exclude seven from the conservative count: one duplicate employer/role, one repeat round, one related-role discussion, one job-fair invitation, one suspected scam, and two platform intro-call invitations. That leaves 14. Add one attended interview recorded in later history but absent from the roster, giving 15. The broader convention includes the three intro/discussion entries, giving 18. Count the two offers separately; an offered interview slot is not a job offer.

Peer review confirmed the conservative arithmetic and identified the three additional records. A second independent review keeps that broader total separately labeled because a related-role discussion is not an established interview invitation. The detailed reconciliation remains private. The [aggregate record](../examples/results.aggregate.json) makes these definitions machine-readable without exposing employers, role-specific dates, or active application statuses.

## What is public

Only aggregate counts and evidence definitions are published in this package. Detailed role titles, individual dates, employer identifiers, private source references, and active pipeline decisions are withheld. The original evidence can be reconciled privately by the owner and reviewer; readers cannot independently authenticate it from this summary alone.

Useful outcomes and recorded failures both shaped the instruction system. It preserves corrections, distinguishes invitations from replies, and keeps final decisions with the user. This case study is evidence of a used personal workflow, not proof of a universal automated hiring service.
