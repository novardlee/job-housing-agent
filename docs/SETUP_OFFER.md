# Paid setup and customization — proposed pilot offer

## The service

The proposed service is owner-led configuration of a personal job-and-housing assistant around your own experience, job targets, interview channels, commute, and housing preferences.

The workflow can help organize job leads, prepare truthful tailored applications, identify interview invitations, create full interview preparation, and compare housing with employment locations. Available automation depends on the assistant host and integrations you authorize.

## Deliverables

- A private canonical profile built from facts you confirm.
- Your search criteria, pay rules, geography, and housing constraints.
- A career-fit evaluation and separate Indeed/ZipRecruiter profile copy packs.
- One agent prompt with explicit job, invitation, interview, reply, and housing functions.
- An explanation of outcome recording and evidence levels, without claims about the customer's future results.
- A draft job queue and interview-preparation demonstration.
- A correction log and handoff instructions so later sessions use current facts.
- A review of the setup with any unsupported functions or required subscriptions identified.

## Scope

One person's configuration and initial draft-mode handoff. It does not include employer submissions, employer-facing messages, rental applications, ongoing monitoring, custom connector development, or a guaranteed job or apartment. Those would need separately agreed scope.

Customers control final applications and communications. They should not send account passwords, government identifiers, or private documents through public GitHub issues.

## Price and availability

Pilot pricing, turnaround, support allowance, and payment channel are not yet set. To ask about setup or commercial permission, use email (novardlee@gmail.com) or the owner's GitHub profile ([github.com/novardlee](https://github.com/novardlee)). Do not send personal documents, passwords, or account details in a first message or a public issue. This is a service description, not an active checkout or a commitment to fulfill orders.

Before accepting a customer, agree the deliverables, required host/integrations, price, delivery date, revision allowance, data handling, and support scope in writing.

## How to test demand

Show the fictional demonstration and ask interested people which part they need most. Record qualified interest separately from paid bookings. Start with one bounded pilot after confirming delivery capability; use feedback to revise the offer before building a hosted product.
