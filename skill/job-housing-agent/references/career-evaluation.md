# Career evaluation — explicit function

The owner confirmed that career evaluation belongs to this project. Inspected sources include `_fit_analysis.py`, `_famanalysis.py`, the canonical profile, and career/housing cross-reference and offer-decision sections. Private scripts were read as context, not executed or copied into this release.

## Purpose

Compare career paths and individual roles against verified experience, realistic next steps, growth evidence, compensation, commute, housing, and user priorities. This is distinct from listing discovery or writing interview answers.

## Assessment

| Dimension | Required evaluation |
|---|---|
| Direct fit | Work actually performed, with evidence |
| Adjacent fit | Transferable skills and the unproven next step |
| Stretch | Missing required tenure, credentials, system ownership, or seniority |
| Career direction | Relevance to chosen field and plausible next roles |
| Growth | Employer-stated training/promotion versus speculation |
| Pay | Actual posting/offer figures, independently of fit |
| Practical feasibility | Shift, commute, housing assumptions, resources, and deadlines |
| Observed response | Role-family interview/offer history, with incomplete-data limits |

The original scripts classify titles into direct/adjacent/stretch families. Preserve that as a heuristic, not a validated hiring predictor. Read the posting's actual requirements; title keywords alone cannot establish eligibility. Do not turn an old script's profile wording into a new fact.

Before proposing a higher pay threshold, measure how many historically interview-producing roles it would exclude and state the count and data limits. Higher pay requirements may narrow opportunities; do not assume a higher cutoff improves the observed interview rate. Keep that analysis separate from changing the user's approved policy.

## Output and boundaries

Return a career memo: verified strengths, realistic role families, gaps, possible training options, evidence-backed role/offer comparison, growth and practical tradeoffs, unknowns, recommendations, and user decisions needed. An immediate bridge role may differ from a long-term career choice. Present that tradeoff without silently changing search filters or overriding a declined offer.

Evaluation does not authorize enrollment, spending, employer messages, applications, or acceptance. Do not promise promotion, predict hiring probability from a keyword label, or invent qualifications to make a role fit.
