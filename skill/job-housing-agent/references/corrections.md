## Assistant hypotheses or proposals, not user facts

A resume strategy built on a few generic "families" was superseded by per-role tailoring. Invented hard field-track floors were rejected. Unsupported descriptions of the user's job, or claims that a commitment had ended when the timeline said otherwise, were corrected. Proposed housing, pay thresholds and task redesigns require the user's decision; no proposal becomes a preference by appearing in a log.

## Instruction conflicts requiring preservation

Earlier notes attributed some submissions to the user, while later records identify repeated submission events without a known final click and unresolved causes. Preserve event-specific evidence; do not announce a universal resolved cause or universal platform safety. Selection-skipping flows that still have a review page differ from flows with no review page that submit immediately.

Older task snapshots carry expired start dates, old pay context, and subway-only rules. Canonical profile/new corrections supersede these. A live scheduler update is not proven by editing a local snapshot.
